# Food images for the personal evening

Three new static assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool on September 3,
2026 and copied into the project as WebP files. They illustrate fictional menu dishes; the
images are not evidence of ingredients or allergy safety. The structured menu declarations
remain authoritative. Original PNGs are preserved in the local generated-images folder.

| Asset | Subject |
| --- | --- |
| `public/art/menu-risotto.webp` | Mushroom risotto with browned mushrooms, parsley and olive oil |
| `public/art/menu-chickpea.webp` | Lemon chickpea salad with cucumber, tomato and parsley |
| `public/art/menu-orzo.webp` | Tomato and basil orzo with roasted tomatoes and olive oil |

## Prompt set

Each image used this common direction: “A professional natural editorial food photograph for
a fictional Mediterranean restaurant menu. Three-quarter overhead view, dish centered and
entirely within frame, delicious realistic food texture, soft side window light, warm taupe
linen background, restrained olive green and warm cream palette. Quiet premium restaurant
photography, no text, no branding, no hands, no collage. Horizontal 3:2 composition.”

- **Risotto subject:** One shallow handmade off-white ceramic bowl of creamy mushroom risotto,
  visible browned sliced mushrooms, fresh flat parsley, black pepper, olive oil sheen.
  No nuts, no avocado, no meat.
- **Salad subject:** A shallow handmade off-white ceramic bowl of lemon chickpea salad, visible
  chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, flat parsley and lemon dressing, olive oil sheen. No nuts,
  no avocado, no meat.
- **Orzo subject:** A shallow handmade off-white ceramic bowl of tomato and basil orzo pasta,
  visible small rice-shaped pasta, slow-roasted cherry tomatoes, fresh basil leaves, glossy
  olive oil. No nuts, no avocado, no meat, no cheese.

The generated images were inspected and encoded as WebP for the web UI. No real restaurant
or photographer is represented by these illustrations.
